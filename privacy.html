Privacy Policy

Effective 6 November 2026

This policy describes the information Elevra LLC receives when you message or comment on an Instagram account we operate automated messaging for, how that information is used, and how to have it removed.

1Scope

This policy applies to the automated messaging system connected to Instagram accounts operated by Elevra LLC, including @fazeyx3. It applies when you send a direct message to one of those accounts, or comment on one of their posts or reels.

It does not govern Instagram itself. Your use of the Instagram platform is subject to Meta's own policies.

2Information we receive

Instagram transmits the following when you message or comment.
InformationPurpose

Instagram usernameSupplied by Instagram with comment events

Message, comment and media identifiersReplying to the correct item and preventing duplicates
TimestampsConversation ordering and messaging window compliance
Email address, where voluntarily providedFollow-up correspondence at your request

We do not receive or request your password. We do not collect your location, your contact list, or information from your Instagram profile beyond the username and user ID attached to your message.

3How the information is used

  • To send an automated reply, including any resource or link requested by keyword.
  • To send a pre-written follow-up reply where your message is not a keyword request.
  • To retain your position in an automated exchange so that follow-up replies remain coherent.
  • To correspond by email, only where you have voluntarily supplied an address.

We do not sell your information, disclose it to advertisers, or use it to construct advertising profiles.

4Automated messaging

Replies from these accounts are sent automatically from pre-written text. You are not corresponding with a person unless expressly stated.

Where a post invites you to comment or message a keyword, that post states that the reply is sent automatically.

No reply is generated by artificial intelligence. Replies are selected by fixed rules according to the keyword you sent and the stage of the exchange. The content of your messages is not transmitted to any language model or other artificial intelligence service.

You may stop the automation at any time by replying to that effect, or by blocking the account.

6Retention

Message records are retained for up to twelve months to support follow-up enquiries and maintain a record of correspondence. Conversation state, meaning your position in an automated exchange, is retained for up to seven days after your most recent message and is then deleted automatically. Email addresses are retained until you request removal.

7Your right to deletion

You may request deletion of all information we hold about you, at any time and without giving a reason. Full instructions are set out on the data deletion page. Requests are completed within thirty days and confirmed by email.

8Children

These accounts and this automation are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us using the details below and it will be deleted.

9Changes to this policy

Where the handling of your information changes materially, this page will be updated and the effective date revised accordingly.
