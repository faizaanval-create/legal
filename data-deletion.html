Data Deletion

Effective 5 November 2026

If you have messaged or commented on an Instagram account operated by Elevra LLC, you may request deletion of all information we hold about you. No reason is required.

1How to submit a request

  1. Email xiwahtesting@gmail.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request.
  2. Include the Instagram username you used when you messaged or commented.
  3. Send from any email address. We will reply to that address.

Alternatively, send a direct message to the Instagram account requesting deletion.

2What is deleted

  • Your Instagram-scoped user ID and username
  • The text of any message or comment you sent
  • Message, comment and media identifiers, and associated timestamps
  • Any email address you provided
  • Any retained conversation context

3Timeframe

Matching records are deleted within thirty days of receipt. We confirm by email once deletion is complete.

4Limitations

We cannot delete information held by Instagram or Meta, including the messages as they appear in your own Instagram inbox. To remove those, use Instagram's own tools or contact Meta directly.

We also cannot delete records we are legally required to retain, although this is unlikely to apply to routine message records.
